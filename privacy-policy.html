1. Introduction

Welcome to Shift Detailing & Coating LLC. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you how we look after your personal data when you interact with our Facebook Ads or visit our website.

When you interact with our ads (such as Facebook Lead Ads), we may collect:

  • Contact Data: Name, email address, and phone number.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

We do not sell your data. However, we share data with trusted service providers to help us run our business, including:

Depending on your location (such as the EU/UK under GDPR or California under CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Request that we delete your personal data.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications at any time.
